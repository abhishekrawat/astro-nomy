## Attention is a design constraint

Skydiving and interface design meet at a difficult question: how much information is useful when attention is already occupied?

This independent altimeter project explores how a physical device and its companion software can provide altitude information with a deliberately narrow communication role.

This is a sample case-study narrative based on the project’s recorded positioning. The visual is a concept illustration, and the current device state needs to be documented with real photographs and validation evidence.

## One principle, many consequences

<StoryMedia tone="ink" caption="Product principle · numeric altitude information, with decisions remaining with the jumper.">
  <div class="story-principle"><span>The communication boundary</span><p>Altitude,<br/>not instructions.</p></div>
</StoryMedia>

The device should communicate objective altitude information. The jumper retains responsibility for the dive plan, awareness, judgment and every action.

That principle narrows the product language. Numeric altitude has a different role from a message that tells someone what to do. The case study should show how this distinction shaped the interaction and voice assets.

## Beyond the flat mockup

A physical prototype exposes questions that a flat mockup can hide: what is readable at a glance, what happens when a connection disappears, and how a person knows whether information is current.

The portfolio story can follow one decision across the device, companion interface and implementation. For example, show how a state is communicated consistently rather than presenting unrelated screenshots of each surface.

## The build, the check, the revision

AI assistance is part of the development process. Its contribution should be visible alongside the design direction, implementation choices and verification performed by the maker.

Describe one concrete loop: a constraint, an implementation attempt, a failed check and the revision. That is stronger evidence than a list of tools or generated code volume.

## What a prototype can prove

An interface prototype demonstrates intent. A successful firmware build establishes compilation in a recorded configuration. A bench test establishes behaviour under its documented conditions. Field validation is a separate level of evidence.

**This portfolio page does not establish operational readiness or flight validation.** The finished story needs real photographs, a recorded bench demonstration and the limits of the checks performed.

The connection to healthcare is the discipline of designing around attention, uncertainty and system state. It is an exploration of human factors, not a claim that a personal prototype is a validated medical or aviation product.
