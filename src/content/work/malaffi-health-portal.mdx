## A record is only the beginning

A health record can contain everything and still leave someone wondering where to look. The design opportunity was to connect a rich clinical system to the questions a patient brings to it.

**In one sentence:** a patient-facing layer for Abu Dhabi’s Health Information Exchange, with comprehension and continuity at its centre.

## Making the information useful

This was my first major healthcare project of its scale.
We had built a solid backend HIE and a robust provider facing portal.
The next big challenge was to have a patient facing app that would leverage on the HIE and help patients to:

- View current and historical medical records
- View and book appointments
- View lab and radiology reports
- See analysis of specific trends (eg. glucose levels over a period of time)
- View medications
- View and compare health profile

## Start with a familiar question

A clinical record is organised to document care. A patient might arrive with “What happened at my last visit?” That difference is a useful starting point for the interface.
